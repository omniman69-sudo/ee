=== Milk Soda

**Ingredients**

* Coke, Pepsi, or any black soda
* Milk, preferably full cream or toned

**Optional**

* Ice cream

**Preparation**

Add milk first, preferably full cream or toned, then slowly add the soda to see it getting mixed. It's so peaceful to watch, like yin and yang.

If you want, add ice cream on top. Vanilla usually tastes good.

If you want it more watery, add less milk. I like it thick.
