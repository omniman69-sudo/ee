# This is just for reference. Users are expected to deep dive into these topics themselves.

<<< Protests >>>

• Avoid Bluetooth.
• Avoid cellular networks when possible.
• Use Airplane Mode.
• Consider a Faraday bag.
• Use privacy-focused communication protocols.

<<< Privacy >>>

• Hide your face and identifying details in nudes.
• Avoid showing fingerprints or other identifying features.
• Avoid biometrics for device or password-manager unlocking if possible.
• Use a hardware security key or strong password instead.

<<< Devices >>>

• Buy devices with unlockable bootloaders.
• Prefer Linux where possible.

<<< Backups >>>

• Keep local backups.
• Encrypt sensitive data before storing it elsewhere.
• Don't rely entirely on cloud storage.

<<< Browser >>>

• Use Firefox + uBlock Origin.
• Learn about data and ad poisoning.

<<< OPSEC >>>

• Never share controversial opinions, political views, or sensitive content without anonymity or encryption.
• On platforms like Instagram or Reddit, you could face legal trouble depending on where you live or become a target of harassment because your OPSEC sucks.
• Keep your OPSEC in mind.

<<< Torrenting >>>

• Always use a VPN while torrenting.
• Keep your OPSEC good.
• Don't rely on weak enforcement. One mistake can make your life a lot harder.
