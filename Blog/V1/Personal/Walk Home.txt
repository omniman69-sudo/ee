# Walk Home

I don't really look up at the sky, but while I was coming from college, a light cold wind was touching my face. It was perfect.

The environment had the right amount of heat, cold, and humidity. It felt refreshing, but when I looked up at the sky, the whole sky was filled with wires, long and thick, especially the ones which are cut and just hanging on other wires while coming on my face. It's irritating. Not only that, a lot of trees are also completely covered in wires.

It completely ruined my fucking day.

These society fuckers have also had 3 cameras attached to a short pole, creating even more mess.

I always wondered who is in charge of these cameras because one is right outside of a school.
The scope of those cameras being used for bad stuff will not shock me.

I have also noticed another thing. In my area, everyone uses a motor to pull water, and every time someone gets a new motor installed, that building gets raided and they try their hardest to pull at least 1 motor out of the cage.

When mine was stolen for the first time, it burned me from inside.

A lot of killing happened in front of my eyes. I just wanted to find that guy or that group of motor mafias, but then my father stole electricity when I was a kid, soo how can I counter it in my mind?

I think I need to be like those people who have no right and wrong. If it works for me, everyone can go and fuck themselves.

I have a bad habit of putting myself in people's shoes all the time.

I should do it less, but it's automatic.
