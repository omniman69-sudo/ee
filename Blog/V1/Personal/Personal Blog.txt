# Personal Blog

A lot of people irl who read my blog ask why I don't make use of AI in my personal blogs like I do in my dream blogs. My answer is simple:

Because I want my personal blogs to have the mistakes I make. It has to feel written by me. There should not be a change of words or a change of the meaning of the sentence, although I read my dream blog and look for not having these problems, but you got the point.

Mistakes are what make my personal blog personal and relatable.

Edit — I write blogs for myself, for my own satisfaction, not to earn money. I want stuff that has filled my brain to have a space so I can think more.
