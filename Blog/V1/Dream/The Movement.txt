# The Movement

For some reason I don’t remember the dream where my physical IRL body moves, but I notice my movement when I am seeing the dream as well as when I am half awake and half asleep. Also, sometimes I can hear outside sounds in my dream. Sometimes that outside sound integrates with the dream, like that one time I was asleep in the sleep and an alarm clock (red color) which I do not have IRL started making the same sound my Android clock makes. I woke up from sleep, looked at that alarm clock, and woke up again. And sometimes the noise IRL is so powerful that I break free from the dream. I remember it, but it starts to fade away while I’m waking up.  

Well, till now the movements I had in my body were:  

1. Movement of thumb or index finger / both upside down or reverse — maybe due to I watch a LOT of Instagram reels (mainly left hand).  

2. Movement of whole body like a flick.  

3. Movement of right leg like a flick.  

4. Movement of right hand thumb in a circle. It can be because of Android games, but I use the right thumb for shooting and left for movement.
