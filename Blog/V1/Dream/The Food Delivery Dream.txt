# The Food Delivery Dream

In this dream, I was in my house and packed some food in a plastic container. I left the house, and when I left, I was shirtless. Then I went on the road to deliver this food to my close uncle barefoot.
I got there and waited for him outside his building, but he did not come, so I went inside and took the stairs to the door. I saw a family member there with my uncle. She was facing backward, but my uncle’s and my eyes got locked in, and I ran down the stairs as fast as I could. Then the dream ended.

In real life, I go to my uncle to deliver food, but because of some beef between members, I have to do it a little secretly.
