# The Three Sisters Dream

In this dream, I was in a house with three girls. I think they were sisters, and I have seen their faces before. I know that, but I do not remember their faces after waking up.
So, in the whole house, I was running behind them, trying to find them, and then I would create a gun shape with my hand and shoot at them, basically getting caught. I kept doing this the whole dream.
In the end, I was behind a bed, and two sisters were in front of me laughing. My hand the whole time was in front of them in the shape of a gun. Then from the right side, another sister came from another room, and then I did the shooting animation to her too, and she was also laughing. I think I was laughing too.
It was a pretty wholesome dream. I loved it. It felt refreshing.
Another thing is that I saw it at 9 PM while I was sleeping in a bad position, and when I woke up, I wanted to puke. One of the sisters was the saggy oval face girl from **The Grey Door** .
