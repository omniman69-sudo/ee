# The Grey Door

In this dream, I was in Group 1, and we were in a mall-like area collecting supplies. I was in a relationship with a girl. There was a lot of history between the groups, but I forgot all of it. The only thing I remember is that aunt. Fuck her. She always had some kind of issues. Also, there was the sister of my girl.

Now I get teleported to the other group, which was totally fucked. I also had a history there, but I forgot it too. It was a pretty long dream.

So that fucked up group attacks my group, and it was brutal. I can't tell everything, but both the aunt and the leader die in that. There was a guy from the other group who came in front of the girl after she opened that grey door, and that guy had red eyes. So she sprayed water from her gun, and nothing happened. Her gun was green, by the way. But then he shot a load of water on her, except it was acid, so it burned the right side of her body. She started running to the exit, crying and screaming, while he ran behind her. I saw that, so I took a triangular beaker with some pink liquid and threw it at him, but I could not see what happened.

So while all of this was happening, and other stuff was happening in the background, a blue shutter opened, and there was a vehicle connected to multiple cargo boxes. Inside those boxes were people who were dirty and hurt, so we all rushed to the boxes. The lady outside said, “Come everybody, fast,” or something like that.

When I stepped out of that hell and placed my foot on the ground, I felt relief that finally it's fucking over. I was near the cargo, and then it clicked in my brain — where is my girlfriend? So I looked back, and the whole store was almost empty. People were still fighting, and there was pressure from them to go inside.

And suddenly the scene changes, and I am running at night toward a big brown gate alone. It was half open, so I went inside and ran through the red staircase. That staircase was very broken. Sometimes I had to jump, sometimes I had to trust my gut because the metallic base was bending downward when I put pressure on it.

So I get to the last staircase, and when I climbed that small ladder halfway, I shouted. There were six people, including my girlfriend’s sister. When I waved at them, they were very happy seeing me. I had a thought in my mind to make her sister my new girlfriend. (It would be a cheating thought.) So I looked back and saw a fucking line of people, and in that line, I could not see my girlfriend. By the way, we were standing in front of a rocket.

It was a very long dream. Also, the dream cuts, and I wake up with my girlfriend’s sister kissing my chest. (Fuck, this is cheating again.) The lighting in that room was pretty soothing, with sunlight coming from somewhere. The whole scene looked picture-perfect.
And then suddenly, I finally wake up again.

I don't remember the faces of those two girls, but my girlfriend had a crisp jawline, and her sister had a saggy oval face with a lot of puff in it, sort of like a polythene bag.
The aunt who was in our team had red hair. I think it's because I recently saw two girls in my college class having the same red hair color. And the acid guy had a longitudinal, stretched rectangular face.

Also, there was no one I have ever seen in real life. Usually, in dreams, real-life people come in, but in this one, no one was from real life.
