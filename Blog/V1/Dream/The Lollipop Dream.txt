# The Lollipop Dream

In this dream, I was in the house with the girl from **The Wholesome Morning Moment** . She was sitting on a chair, and the environment was pretty yellow and cozy. I was in front of her, and then I went back and grabbed two lollipops. They had white sticks, and the upper circular lollipops were packaged in square plastic. The plastic was red in color and had something on it, maybe ads.
Then I was giving them to her, and while she moved her hand forward to grab one of them, the dream ended.

Then I woke up. I remember there was a lot more happening in that dream, but I had already forgotten it. When I woke up, I suddenly got the knowledge of another dream I had a long time ago, which was kind of related to this one. But I was sleepy, so I did not record that in my notes and just went back to sleep.

When I woke up in the morning, both dreams were completely wiped out of my brain. I am very angry and frustrated right now. I tried very hard to remember, but I just cannot.
