# The Blue Mall Dream

In this dream, I was in a big mall, and the mall had dark blue pillars. I think I was in front of a ward, but then I met someone, and he said, “Go get the medicines.” So I moved back, and then I went from that small staircase to the medicine area.

That medicine area was very futuristic. There was a whole rectangular screen placed vertically, and it was turned off. Everything had blue LED neon light, and it looked very fun. Then I looked at the guy. He looked like a Chinese uncle, and when I asked him for the medicine, he just looked at me, then packed his bags and walked away.

I then went back from that same small staircase, and when I turned back, I saw a very big area with two girls talking and doing something on the phone. There were people around me walking at the side of that long hall. Everything was very blueish, even the walls. They were grey and bluish in color.

So I started walking towards the girls, but then I changed my path and started walking straight. When I got to the end, I saw an elevator, so I pressed the down button, and suddenly a very big tire appeared from that elevator and it was coming towards me. I froze at that moment, and in real life my heartbeat raised, so I pressed the elevator again. This time, the thing that closes the elevator got fucked.

Then I turned left, and I saw another truck tire coming towards me. It was very big, and at that moment I got scared and started running toward the woman. When I came close to them, a person came who had no clothes on except underwear, and then the scene changed.

I was at a beach, and everyone was enjoying themselves. Music was playing, and there was a sunset happening. It was a pretty cool place, and then I talked to that guy, and we also danced.

Suddenly, a wave of sound came. I looked up and saw dark clouds forming, and I also saw a yellow light coming behind the trees. While everyone was enjoying themselves, the underwear guy started running somewhere, so I also started running behind him for some reason.

Then he increased speed and ran as fast as he could. Another wave came, and I thought it was a nuclear explosion, so I also ran as fast as I could. When I looked back, there was nothing. There was no beach anymore. It was a green grass area that we were running in, in the dark.

While we were running, I saw a swimming pool without water, and the guy jumped from it. I also jumped from it, and then the guy started running toward a hole, which was between that grass area. As usual, I started running as well, but this time I ran as fast as I could, like it was a life-or-death situation.

But suddenly I got consciousness. I started waking up, but I did not want to wake up. In that time between waking up and not waking up, I heard some sounds like they were coming from an old TV, like zzzzzz.

Anyways, I got into the hole, and when I got in, I saw that the guy had locked me outside. I started banging on the door, but he just would not open it, and then the dream was over.

When I woke up from the dream, the silence felt pretty good.
