# The Finger Skin Dream

In real life, in hot summer, the top skin of my fingers and toes starts to shed. So I use my teeth, take a part of that skin, and start removing it. Sometimes I do it too much, to the point where it starts to hurt, and I can no longer take it any further. So while the blood is coming out, I remove the skin from that area.

In this dream, there was a hand, which was my hand, and there were no teeth. The background was black, and from the middle of the top part of the index finger, the skin started to stretch in a horizontal line toward the middle. At that point, I felt pain, and throughout the dream I was trying to stretch it beyond that, but I could not because of the pain. Then I woke up.
