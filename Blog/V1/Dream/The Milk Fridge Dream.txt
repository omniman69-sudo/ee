# The Milk Fridge Dream

In this dream, I was in front of my house fridge. I opened it and started to pour milk on the top glass slab, which had a cover with flowers on it, just like in real life. That milk started to drip slowly from behind the fridge, and then it came almost to my leg from behind the vegetable basket.
This whole process made me furious. In the dream, there was an anger and burn in me that’s hard to describe. The anger can be described like when you always tell someone to check if the heater is turned off after a bath, and one day you go inside and see the heater on.
That was the whole night.
