# The Laptop and Phone Dream

In this dream, I was in a room with two people: one was a kid bigger than me, and the other was an aunt. The kid was sitting on a chair, and the aunt was on the bed with a blanket on. I think it was purple, and since it was a blanket, that means it was winter season. The walls had yellow paint on them.
So I asked the woman if I could call someone and if she could lend me her laptop. The woman who had the laptop looked at that kid and gave it to me, but I was just doing random stuff instead of calling, because calling happens on a phone, not a laptop. The laptop was black in color and had Windows 11. It also had a Type-C device connected to it, which I had not seen in real life before, but it looked cool.
So I gave back the laptop, and I came behind the kid. Then I saw a phone beside her. I told her to call someone and that I would not even touch her phone. So she asked me for the number while lifting the phone, and I told her my real-life phone number. And while the call was ringing, I woke up.

I got another dream, but because I slept after seeing that, I totally forgot what the dream was.
