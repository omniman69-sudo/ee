# Three Dreams in One Night

In this dream, it was raining heavily. The aesthetic of that scene was brown, more specifically tree-brown, and everyone was wearing a brown jacket with blue jeans. Some also had cowboy hats on.

I do not remember this part of the dream very clearly, but basically I walked up to a group of guys and talked to them. Then one of them pointed and said, “Go in the store and wait.” So I went down the stairs, got inside, and sat there.

Then the guy came and asked me what I wanted. I said I wanted a loan because, for some reason, I could not get my money out. Then the guy asked what it was for, and I told him I wanted to find someone, and I showed him the photo of that kid.

The guy said these types of cases are common and that the kid would not be found. Then we had a little more discussion, and after that I woke up.

Then I slept again in real life, and this time I was in a jungle. An animal like a tiger was having a race with me, and it was epic. My legs were flying like a bird. But in the middle of the race, a guy came and said, “Go back home.” So the animal and I started going back, and the area around us slowly started to have buildings.

Then I woke up. Not gonna lie, I was very happy while racing.

I slept again, and this time I was in a house. I was sitting beside a girl whose face I do not remember, and in front of us was a small round brown table. We were sitting on the floor. The floor was made of marble, and the house looked expensive.

We were playing some game — I do not remember what — but I was losing continuously. Every time I lost, I kissed both of her cheeks, the bone area between the neck and face on the right side, and the palm of her hand.

And then I woke up.
