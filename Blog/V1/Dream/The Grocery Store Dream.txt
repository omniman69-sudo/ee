# The Grocery Store Dream

In this dream, I was in a grocery store and I was looking for the usual things I buy in real life. The grocery store had a green and white color palette. While I was looking for what I needed, a room suddenly appeared. It was pretty interesting, so I went inside.

When I got in, I saw someone’s house that I had never seen in real life. In a circle, there were some of my friends playing cards. The girl I have a crush on grabbed my hand and made me sit there. I sat with them, playing and laughing, even though I do not know how to play cards.

Then I turned back, and when I did, I saw a skin-colored sofa and a woman in a black dress just staring into my soul. She had very beautiful big black eyes, and she was beautiful in general. I do not remember her face, though i saw her in **The Grey Door** the saggy oval face girl . But then I felt very weird looking at her, so I stood up.

The girl I have a crush on asked me where I was going. I said I was going home; it was time. So I opened my bag and checked everything in it. In real life, I also check my bag a lot of times because you never know when a guy might get a knife and just tear the bag from behind, taking your stuff.

Anyways, I checked, and everything was there. Then I moved forward. When I got out of that room, everything was scattered. All the vegetables and all the food products were just scattered everywhere. It was chaos there.

Then I saw a child. I gave him space to go to his father. His father looked at me, and I looked at him, and then I moved on.

Seeing all of this chaos, I wanted to go outside the grocery store, so I moved straight toward the exit. I stopped in between and checked my bag again to make sure everything was in there, and everything was in there. And as soon as I came close to the door, suddenly I got blacked out, and then the dream was over.
