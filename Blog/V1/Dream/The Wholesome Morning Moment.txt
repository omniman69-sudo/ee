# The Wholesome Morning Moment

I do not remember the whole dream, but in this dream, the people I know were near me, and we were at my mother’s brother’s house.
There was this one famous girl I wanted to talk to, so while she was going somewhere, I approached her and asked, “Would you like to click some pictures with me?”
And she said, “In the morning.”
But then I said, “You will leave in the morning.”
Then brother’s wife said something about both of us, and all the people started laughing, including us.
Pretty wholesome moment.
