# The Three Smiles

In this dream I was in a house which was not mine, possibly from a movie. I was coming towards this girl whose face I don’t remember anymore.  

There was this white door with 4 mirrors separated by a “+” behind the stairs, and in front was a round table. Everything was decorated in white.  

Suddenly the door started busting. I was going towards the door and saw 3 children in the dark outside wearing yellow shirts and sandy half jeans. They were smiling.  

Then I saw a black revolver on the head of 1 child from the right, and the guy shot 3 bullets through 3 heads.  

I ran to open the door but there was nothing. While I was looking, I saw green grass, a lamp, and another house—while listening to this high-pitch scream which woke me up.
