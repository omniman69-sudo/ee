# The Brown Book and the Robots

In this dream, I was in a room, and the atmosphere of the room was yellowish-brown. Suddenly, a book came into my hand. It was brown in color, and something was written on it in golden color.

When I opened the book, the pages were brown in color, and I could also smell the fragrance of the pages that usually comes from an old book. Then I opened a part of the book and saw a picture of a brown robot. Then I went to another page, and there was another robot. Then I saw another robot, and then another robot, but this robot had a chainsaw with it.

Then suddenly the scene changed, and I was in a plain field. There was sunlight, and there was a surface full of grass. I started running for some reason, and then I was just running as fast as I could. When I looked behind, the first robot was just running behind me, so my heartbeat in real life started to increase and I ran even faster.
Then these robots started coming from all sides while I was running, and eventually I got caught, and there was black.

Then the scene was again the same. There was sunlight and green grass, but this time I was behind a bush. It was a square area with fences, and I was hiding from something. When I peeped from the bush, I saw that second robot, and it started, and then it to swami, but I was hiding from him.

Then I started running, but I got stuck in the fence, and then he killed me, I think, because then it was black, and then I woke up. My heartbeat was racing very high, and I could hear staff from someone. It was a male. Then suddenly there was complete silence.

Then I went back to sleep, and this time there were dark clouds, and there was just a flat brown area. I was running as fast as I could, and they all were coming from the front and from behind. It was a very tense chaos.

I also got the ability to jump very high, so I was running and jumping, dodging every single one of them. It was the chainsaw one this time, and I was just running, running, running, dodging every single one of them.

But then suddenly I saw a city with big buildings, and I was very happy, so I ran, ran, ran. Suddenly, the four chainsaw people robots started coming towards me with a wide alien-like face with eyes and two hands, and it was in a rocky shape like that rock Pokemon.

I was going to dodge him, but he was in the air, and it was hard to dodge him. But somehow I dodged him from under. Because I dodged him from under, I had no time to jump, and I got got by four robots. Then there was black, and I woke up.

When I woke up, my heartbeat was racing very high, and I was sweaty.
