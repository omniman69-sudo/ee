# The Girl Who Kept Forgetting Me

In this dream, I was in a big field filled with green grass and neon lighting everywhere. There were people roaming around, and it was nighttime. Everyone was having fun—it felt very peaceful. I was also having fun there. I was jumping, roaming around, talking to people.

Then I went to this class and saw this girl. I talked to her, but then someone came and started taking me out, so I grabbed her hand and ran. We had fun—not in a sex way, just general talking and jumping. That's all I remember. I don't even remember what we talked about, but I remember we talked anyway.

There was a pool in between the yard, and there were different kinds of pouches lying around somewhere—blue, somewhere red. Some had different designs; some had amoeba shapes. I grabbed the blue one and asked her to go inside. She got inside of it, and then that pouch inflated. I could not see her inside, but then I was just tossing it upwards and catching it—that's all I was doing.

Then the next day happened. It was night again. I remember everything, but she did nothing, nor the people around. So I went to her again and did the same thing again, but this time we had a lot more fun. Again, I don't remember what we exactly did, but we did a lot. This time, not only blue neon was there, but there was yellow, red, and green as well.

Then the next night happened again. She forgot everything, and again everything happened again. In the dream, I felt pretty lonely because she was just forgetting me, and it was very frustrating.

So then the next night happened again, and this time we got into our dancing area. That dancing area had light blue vibes, and the floor was of metal. Everything there was built of metal, and everyone was dancing. It was so good. After dancing, we talked a lot, and then we got to the same area—the blue lake and all the pouches. So I told her to get in the pouch, and she did. But this time, while I was throwing her upwards, there were many electrical lines attached to a transformer, and by mistake, while I was throwing her upwards, I threw her near the transformer. Because that pouch had water, that pouch burst, and I think she died.

Then the next day happened. This time it was day, and I went to the field. The field was empty, and there were patches of grass, but mostly it was sand. So I went in and sat on the sandy floor at the sides of that area and just felt lonely. I was hoping that she was with me.

When I woke up, I also felt lonely, even though I knew it was only just a dream. There was a lot that happened other than this story in that dream. Like at one point, she got very annoyed with me, and her smile was mesmerizing. I don't remember her face, but I think she had black hair or brown, something in between. And she had a very curvy figure . She was the same girl from **The Grey Door**  saggy oval face one .

I hope today I get a dream like this or the continuation of this dream because it was too perfect.
