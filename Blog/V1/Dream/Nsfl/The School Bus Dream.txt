# The School Bus Dream

In this dream, I was in a school bus, and the roads were pretty empty, with small kids around. I was seated there, and the bus was moving. There were random noises everywhere, like children make. We were all wearing yellow shirts with dark blue pants and an ID card as well.

And suddenly, I was in the bus with another girl, and everyone disappeared except the guy who was driving. Then he left the girl at her destination, and then he reversed the bus. While reversing, he tried to go forward into the grass area, and then he stopped it on the slope.

So I asked him, “What the fuck are you doing?” He said he was turning it and we were going back to school. So I looked behind, and I remembered that my house was pretty close to that main road. So I told him, “Just get me to the main road and I’ll go by myself.” He said no, so I was just sitting there.

Then suddenly he decided not to reverse anymore and to go straight on the path. He increased the speed, and he was just going on the right side. I looked and saw a lot of balls made of sand, but I did not care. But there was a line, a wall of sand.

Then we got to this area where there was a pit of sand, and beside it, at a lower level, there was a whole sandy area. So the bus driver decided to go full speed and jump the bus to that lower area. So he did. Then there were some more sandy, rough, bumpy areas with patches of different tires on them.

Now suddenly the scene changes, and I am one of the construction workers that were working in that sand bed. I saw the bus going away from my direction, so I notified others, and everyone just started running and started trying to stop him, but he did not listen.

Then we got a camera, and then I became the two construction workers who were running from that area. While they were running, a dinosaur came, which only had a skeleton on it, and he ate the guy who was on top of me. And then as soon as he started to leave, I started running, and he recognized me running and also ate me.

Then the scene changes again, and now I am in a costume, and I am asking my friend to open the door. The costume seems very sci-fi. So he opens the door, and from the sandy area we go into some abandoned building.

There, while being in the mask, I asked to stand for that dinosaur and the others, and the scan gets completed. It turns out he was at the edge of the side, so I took out my sniper and came in front of him and shot him, and then ran back. The sound of the sniper was the same as the sound of that sniper from Apex Legends Mobile (Kraber).

Then he came in front of me in that hall, so I sniped him again and then knifed him again. But it turns out that the dinosaur was just getting its energy. I was targeting the eye, and whenever I hit the eye, a blue line came at the side of the eyes, and it seemed like it was absorbing that energy before shooting.

Then, after those two bullets, the AI that was in my suit converted the whole area into some school. And the dinosaur had a skin on it; it did not have any of that stuff in real life in the dream, but the system created this whole projection for me so it would be easy for me to kill that fucker.
And then while I shot those two bullets, some people in my real life woke me up, and the dream ended.
