# The Havoc at My Uncle’s House Dream

In this dream, I was at my mother’s brother’s house. His wife and daughter were there. The daughter opened the door, and a guy with glasses, a black shirt, and jeans was standing outside. He was taller than me and had a good body — not very muscular, but well-built. Some conversation happened, and she pushed him away. I came outside and pushed him as well, and he rolled on the stairs and lay there in pain. Then we both went back inside.

The scene changed, and we were on the balcony. I saw him again, so I started throwing rocks at him. Another guy was with him, and behind them was a black car. He said something that made me look across the road, and I saw a woman. I took a very big rock and threw it at her. It hit her on the head. I do not know if she died, but she definitely was not going to open her eyes for a while. Then we both started throwing rocks at him.

I could see anger in his eyes. He started running toward the building and pressed the doorbell frantically. This time, her mother opened the door. He came inside and sat on the sofa furiously. I also came and sat on the sofa. Both of the women were not there. I pulled out a glock and started shooting at him, but every bullet missed.

I removed the top black part of the pistol and replaced it with a purple one. This time, he had a phone lying on his thighs with no back cover. I could see the battery and other components. I shot at the battery, and there was a spark. Then her mother came with a futuristic gun, inserted it into the wooden back part of the long sofa, and turned it on. The guy started screaming, melting and disintegrating, and I just watched.

The scene changed again. I saw under the bed that there was an underground metro station. His melted body was lying on the track. A silver-white train approached, the atmosphere around it also silver, and the train crushed him. The camera followed the train until I saw the next station on a yellow digital board shaped like an open parenthesis { ( }, with the open part below.

Then the dream ended.
