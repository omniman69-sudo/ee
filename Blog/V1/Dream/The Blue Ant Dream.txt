# The Blue Ant Dream

This dream was very long, but because the environment around me was so cozy, I forgot almost all of it after waking up.
In this dream, I was in a wooden house, and the whole night I was just killing these blue glowing ants while these fuckers ate everything, and I was not able to stop them. I screamed, yelled, used curse words, used my hands and different tools, but I just could not get rid of those fuckers.
I talked to people, they helped me, and then they disappeared as well.
At the end, there were too many to control, and I think they consumed me as well. That is just an assumption, because I do not remember much of the dream.
In real life, I have a trail of red ants always getting the food in the kitchen, and I am scared of them getting onto my T-shirt while I forget they exist from the edge of the slab.
Those blue ants were beautiful, by the way. I have never seen anything like that.
