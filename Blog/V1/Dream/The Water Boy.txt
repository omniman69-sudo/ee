# The Water Boy

In real life, I get my water from a water boy, and he charges 25 rupees per bottle. So what happens is that sometimes he accidentally spills some water outside the bottle onto the ground. But it has happened so many times now that it’s become very annoying.

So in this dream, he did it again, but this time he spilled way too much. A very big chunk of water was just on the ground, and he had the audacity to not even look me in the eye. He just kept filling the bottle.
This time, I did not hold back. I just started verbally fighting with the guy. I remember something that I said to him, but I cannot tell it because it’s very personal.
And then he left, and by the way, I did not pay him.

So that was the whole dream. The whole 8 hours, I was just screaming, lol.
