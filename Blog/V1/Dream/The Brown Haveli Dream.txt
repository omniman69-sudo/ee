# The Brown Haveli Dream

In this dream, I entered a big haveli-type house, and everything there was very brownish. The glasses were yellowish-brown, and the stones used had a brown or facial color on them. There was a big red carpet, and above it were slices of long red cloth strings attached to the sides of that area. In the middle, all those strings were attached to a red circular thing hanging above.

So I came in and started roaming around, trying to find someone to talk to. By the way, there was no roof in the middle of that haveli. So I found a guy, and we talked. I do not remember about what.

Then we went to this aunty who was wearing a saree with red and yellow colors in it, and we asked her where the girl was. She pointed toward one of the upper rooms. So we both started going there, and in between we saw this very beautiful aunty whose saree color was purple and white. She had curly hair and a curvy body, and she went into a room.

My lustful brain, for some reason, started peeking through a hole that was in one of the bricks. Then she went into a squatting position and just started staring at me. In the dream, it did not feel weird, but now that I think about it, I think that was a trap because purple in my brain usually indicates poison.

So we started marching toward the room after going through the staircase to the first floor. Outside that room, I saw a guy giving money to a girl and, I think, her husband. The girl was wearing a pink saree, and she had covered her face.

Now the scene changes, and I am sitting on a sofa, and there is a table. An uncle and aunty were there, whom I do not know, and there was tea in cups on the brown circular table. Some talk happened there which I do not remember, but I do know that the same or maybe a different pink saree girl came out, and I said something in my mind in the dream.
And then the dream ended.
By the way, whatever people I saw in this dream, I have never seen any of them in real life.
